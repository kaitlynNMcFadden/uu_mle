survival lab
-------------
- at first, people might assume that everyone dies, but that is imputing that they will die
- next wrong step, throwing out all the people that passed away, but then you are throwing away certain results
- so what do you do,
    - if you died during the study: take the logs of the time they lived for the likelihood (the first LL)
    - if you survivied the study: take the prob that you exceed that time of the end of the study, 1-p people (second LL)
        - this second one is called the right censored likelihood (survival data)

could just do the prob of people who survivied and those who don't, but it won't help
us decide which therapy would be better, or any other non-straightforward questions

example:
- 2 choices: a consistent probability of death but its uncertain, or live 3 years and die
- in this world, we want a good model to figure out the prob of when people will die 

overview:
- we have 2 different LL functions for 2 different groups, people who died and those who did not die
- we have a combination of the 2
